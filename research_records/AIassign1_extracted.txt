                              AI Assignment #1
                     CISC3024 Pattern Recognition

                                                     September, 2026

   This course has three AI assignments to train students to use AI tools/agent to
efficiently search and implement algorithms to solve real-world pattern recognition
problems.

   AI Assignment # 1 aims to find a recent algorithm of Deep CNN or Deep Au-
toencoder with an application in computer vision and pattern recognition. You are
expected to use an AI tool (like Deepseek or ChatGPT) or an AI agent to do ev-
erything for you, including, but not limited to, algorithm searching, programming,
and even report writing.

   A report with detailed steps on how you did this assignment should be submitted
online via UMMoodle next week and then go through a Turnitin similarity check.

   Note: You are NOT allowed to write any code by yourself !
